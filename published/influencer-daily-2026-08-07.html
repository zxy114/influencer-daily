13. AI 扩散慢是因为我们在用「实验室黑话」
🧠 思维
Been thinking about why AI diffusion has been slow. It's because we are asking users to understand our nerdy lab speak. We greet them with a blank window and ask them to write a prompt. Then they have to pick a model, decide whether they need an agent, and somehow know what...
来源：@realmadhuguru ⭐⭐⭐⭐
🎯 如何应用：自己做内容/产品时，避免"prompt / agent / model / context"这些黑话直接露给用户。用"让 AI 帮你做 X"这种动词结构代替。下次写推文/教程/落地页文案先做一次"小白读一遍"测试。
