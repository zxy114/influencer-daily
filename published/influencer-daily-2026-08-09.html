11. AI Agent 落地最大瓶颈不是模型（3 个避坑点）

#思维 #工作流
"The biggest bottleneck to building a great AI agent isn't the model. It's when teams: → Bury their agent in too much context → Don't give it tools to find what it needs → Try to cover too much vs. nail a few core use cases."
来源：@petergyang
⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐
